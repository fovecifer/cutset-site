Support

Cutset is a logic puzzle about shutting down industrial pipe networks.

For questions, bug reports or purchase issues, email fovecifer@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version and, if relevant, the level number.

Restoring the Expansion Permit

If levels 21–100 are locked on a device where you already bought the Expansion Permit, open EXPANSION PERMIT from the main menu and tap RESTORE PURCHASE. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase.

Progress across devices

Progress syncs through iCloud when iCloud is enabled for Cutset on each device. Sync timing is controlled by iOS and can take a few minutes.

支持

Cutset 是一款围绕工业管网展开的逻辑谜题。

如有问题、错误报告或购买相关事宜，请发邮件至 fovecifer@gmail.com， 并附上设备型号、iOS 版本，以及相关的关卡编号。

恢复「扩建许可」

如果已购买「扩建许可」，但某台设备上第 21–100 关仍未解锁，请在主菜单进入 扩建许可，点 恢复购买。请确认登录的是购买时使用的同一个 Apple 账户。

多设备进度

在各台设备上为 Cutset 开启 iCloud 后，进度会通过 iCloud 同步。 同步时机由 iOS 决定，可能需要几分钟。
