Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Cutset does not collect, store or share any personal data. The developer receives no information about you or how you play.

What stays on your device

Your level progress and settings are saved on your device. The game contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and it does not connect to any server run by the developer.

Apple services

If you choose to use them, the following Apple services are handled entirely by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy:

  • iCloud — syncs your level progress between your Apple devices.
  • Game Center — records achievements.
  • App Store — processes the Expansion Permit purchase. The developer never sees your payment details.

Children

Because no personal data is collected, none is collected from children.

Changes and contact

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: fovecifer@gmail.com.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 25 日

Cutset 不收集、不存储、也不分享任何个人数据。开发者不会获得任何关于你或你如何游玩的信息。

保存在你设备上的内容

关卡进度与设置保存在你的设备上。游戏不含广告、不含统计分析、不含任何第三方 SDK， 也不会连接开发者运营的任何服务器。

Apple 服务

以下 Apple 服务如你选择使用，完全由 Apple 依照 Apple 隐私政策处理：

  • iCloud：在你的 Apple 设备之间同步关卡进度。
  • Game Center：记录成就。
  • App Store：处理「扩建许可」的购买。开发者无法看到你的付款信息。

儿童

由于不收集任何个人数据，因此也不会收集儿童的个人数据。

变更与联系

本政策如有变更，会在本页公布并更新生效日期。 如有疑问：fovecifer@gmail.com。
